# Project Brief — Reset Men Salon Rebuild

## 1. Project Overview
- **Client**: Reset Men Salon
- **Location**: Business Bay, Dubai, United Arab Emirates
- **Domain**: [https://resetmensalon.ae/](https://resetmensalon.ae/)
- **Category**: High-End Luxury Men's Grooming, Barbering & Japanese Head Spa

Reset Men Salon is an elevated sanctuary for modern gentlemen seeking precision haircuts, beard architecture, skin rejuvenation, and bespoke Japanese Head Spa therapies in Dubai's premier business district.

---

## 2. Core Objectives
1. **Elevate Brand Perception**: Transform the digital experience to match the physical salon's ultra-luxurious, calm, and architectural atmosphere.
2. **Drive High-Value Bookings**: Streamline the booking journey via WhatsApp concierge and digital appointment scheduling.
3. **Establish Local Search Dominance**: Outrank competitor salons across Business Bay, Downtown Dubai, DIFC, and the wider UAE for key grooming terms.
4. **Deliver Flawless Performance**: Guarantee instant page loads (<1.2s LCP), zero CLS, and seamless mobile interactions.
5. **Decouple Content Management**: Empower content updates (services, pricing, journal articles) via a headless CMS layer without code redeployment.

---

## 3. Target Audience
- **The Executive / Professional**: High-net-worth individuals, executives, and founders working in DIFC, Downtown, and Business Bay requiring punctual, precision grooming.
- **The Modern Connoisseur**: Discerning men seeking advanced scalp therapy (Japanese Head Spa), facials, and tailored self-care.
- **The Style Conscious Resident / Traveler**: Locals and international visitors looking for Dubai's highest rated grooming experience.

---

## 4. Key Salon Pillars & USPs
- **Master Craftsmanship**: Barbers with decades of combined European and Middle Eastern mastery.
- **Signature Japanese Head Spa**: 15-step sensory scalp detoxification, microscopic follicle analysis, herbal misting, and acupressure.
- **Private & Refined Ambience**: Acoustic privacy, minimalist luxury materials (matte slate, brushed bronze, smoked walnut).
- **Comprehensive Menu**: Hair, Beard, Facial, Scalp Treatments, Nails, Waxing, and Therapeutic Massages.
