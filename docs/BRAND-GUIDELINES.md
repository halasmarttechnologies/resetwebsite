# Brand Guidelines — Reset Men Salon

## 1. Brand Identity & Essence
- **Brand Name**: RESET MEN SALON
- **Tagline**: The Architecture of Modern Grooming
- **Location**: Business Bay, Dubai, UAE
- **Tone of Voice**: Sophisticated, Confident, Understated, Precise, Welcoming.

---

## 2. Voice & Tone Principles
- **Elevated, Never Loud**: Use calm, refined vocabulary. Avoid exclamation marks or aggressive sales jargon ("Hurry!", "Best in Dubai!!").
- **Precision & Craft**: Emphasize technique, tailored consultations, organic apothecary products, and Japanese ritual wellness.
- **Respect for Time**: Reflect our clientele's high-efficiency lifestyle — concise booking, dedicated concierge, flawless punctuality.

---

## 3. Imagery & Art Direction
- **Lighting**: Cinematic directional chiaroscuro lighting, soft warm highlights, deep clean shadows.
- **Subjects**: Sharp beard fades, textured scissor cuts, calm Head Spa hydrotherapy waterfalls, clean grooming tools on dark granite.
- **Color Grading**: De-saturated cool tones balanced with warm amber and bronze skin reflections.
- **Avoid**: Stock photo smiles, oversaturated colors, cluttered salon floors, cheesy grooming props.

---

## 4. Logo Usage & Clear Space
- **Primary Mark**: `RESET` uppercase with refined tracking and clean modern serifs/geometric lines.
- **Secondary Mark**: `RESET MEN SALON — DUBAI`
- **Clear Space**: Maintain minimum spacing equal to the height of the letter 'R' around all perimeter boundaries.
- **Placement**: Clean alignment on dark obsidian backdrops with minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio.
